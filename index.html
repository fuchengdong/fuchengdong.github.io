Research

My expertise lies in designing controllable generative models through semantic guidance and hierarchical control mechanisms, with a focus on modular, plug-and-play architectures.

I'm interested in bridging visual understanding and controllable generation in AI systems. Specifically, I wish to use computer vision and multi-modal learning algorithms to develop efficient personalization techniques for diffusion models, enabling rich, fine-grained control over user interactions for customized content creation experiences.

Featured Projects

Selected work on camera control, action-conditioned video, and persistent world generation.

Highlights

Camera-Controlled Generation for Video World Models

A camera-conditioned video world model that treats viewpoint as an explicit control signal. Conditioned on a camera trajectory, it maintains a persistent latent scene state and rolls out spatiotemporally consistent observations, preserving 3D geometry, object identity, and illumination under novel views.

World Action Model

A world action model that generates video from actions. Conditioned on an action sequence, turning control into a persistent visual world rather than a clip played on a fixed path.

2025 – Present

Demo|Case Study

Streaming Video Synthesis with Interactive Physical Conditioning

A novel framework for enabling real-time streaming video generation conditioned on dynamic interactive inputs. The system ensures that motion continuity, temporal coherence, and visual fidelity remain consistent across continuously synthesized frames, from single-prompt initialization to multi-modal interactive control signals.

2025 – Present

Demo|Case Study

Playable Game Worlds via Interactive Video Generation

A playable world-generation system that turns continuous video synthesis into a third-person game experience. Character motion is driven by real-time WASD control while the surrounding environment is generated on the fly, preserving spatial layout, lighting, and temporal coherence—so walking and exploring feel like navigating a persistent world rather than watching a clip.

2025 – Present

Demo|Case Study

A Long-Term Consistent Video World Generation

A real-time world-model that generates continuous video streams while maintaining long-horizon coherence. Designed for interactive, low-latency use, supports real-time updates from user inputs or events while keeping the world consistent—so actions have lasting consequences and the scene remains logically aligned over time.
